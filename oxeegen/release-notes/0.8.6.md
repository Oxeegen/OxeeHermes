- **Choose your model**: Oxeegen's Oxee-max stays the model Oxee Hermes starts with, but you can now
  pick another provider and model. Add its API key, or an OpenAI-compatible endpoint, in
  Settings › Providers.
- **Helper tasks on the right model**: titles, context compression, image reading and the other
  helper tasks each run on the Oxeegen model suited to them. Settings › Model › Auxiliary models ›
  **Reset all to defaults** brings them back.
- **Web search with Brave and Firecrawl**: enter your keys in Settings › Advanced › Tools. With both,
  Brave runs the searches and Firecrawl reads the pages, which saves Firecrawl credits. Exa,
  Parallel, and FAL for images and video, are available there too.
- **The Oxeegen Style Kit**: a built-in skill gives the documents, pages and slides Oxee Hermes
  makes the Oxeegen look: palette, logos, light and dark modes. The Oxeegen theme wears the same
  palette.
- **French everywhere**: the English left in the French app is gone: guided tour, settings help,
  connectors, messaging setup, scheduled-job templates, catalog categories and commands. The app
  also starts straight in your language.
- **A faster Skills page**: it opens about six times faster, and typing in its search no longer
  stalls.
- **The Oxeegen library slide**: the text sits on the left half and the button bottom right. An
  installed skill shows **Installed**, with its on/off switch.
- **Ready as soon as you connect**: the status bar shows the backend ready right after you enter
  your Oxeegen key, instead of minutes later.
- **Settings**: Model, Advanced and Providers are back. The agent's terminal runs on this computer
  only.

### For IT: the per-machine .msi

A new `OxeeHermesBundled-0.8.6-x64.msi` installs Oxee Hermes silently in Program Files for every
user of a computer, by GPO, Intune or by hand. The Oxeegen region and key, and optionally the Brave
and Firecrawl keys, can be given at install: users then have nothing to enter and cannot change
them. `new-oxeegen-mst.ps1` builds one transform per client for GPO deployment. Updates come by
deploying the next .msi. Guide:
[installation-msi.md](https://github.com/Oxeegen/OxeeHermes/blob/main/oxeegen/docs/installation-msi.md)
(in French).

### Install

- **Windows**: download `OxeeHermesBundled-Setup-0.8.6-x64.exe` below and run it, or update from
  Settings › About. The installer is not signed yet: SmartScreen shows a warning, click
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: download the `.dmg` below and drag Oxee Hermes into Applications. The app is not signed
  yet: the first time, right-click it and choose **Open**.
