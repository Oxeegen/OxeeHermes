- **The Oxeegen library**: Capabilities › Skills now opens on the skills Oxeegen publishes and
  reviews, under the Oxeegen logo. Each one shows on its own visual, with its name, description and
  keywords in your language, and installs in one click. Beside it, the other library skills follow
  one another, as thumbnails with their names; click one to bring it up. The first one is
  **Document Assembler**: it assembles your notes, letters and PDFs into a single clean deliverable,
  with a table of contents and page numbers.
- **Library updates**: when Oxeegen publishes a newer version of a library skill you installed,
  Oxee Hermes tells you at startup, and checks again every hour, with an **Update** button. Nothing
  updates without you.
- **One question at a time**: when you install an update, the Updates dialog steps aside while
  Oxee Hermes asks **Install and restart?**, instead of staying open behind it.
- **No Nous Research sign-in**: the status bar no longer offers to sign in to Nous Research.
- **Descriptions in French**: about 700 more skill and plugin descriptions, added to the catalogs
  since 0.8.4, now show in French.
- **Updates in seconds, fixed**: the quick update could sometimes close the app without updating it
  or reopening it. It now waits for the app to have really quit. This update itself goes through the
  full installer; the next ones download only the files that changed.

### Install

- **Windows**: download `OxeeHermesBundled-Setup-0.8.5-x64.exe` below and run it, or update from
  Settings › About. The installer is not signed yet: SmartScreen shows a warning, click
  **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: download the `.dmg` below and drag Oxee Hermes into Applications. The app is not signed
  yet: the first time, right-click it and choose **Open**.
